About Us

English Conquest is a student-led educational platform designed to make SPM English learning more engaging and effective. We provide curated notes in visual form, so learners can absorb information quickly and recall it better during exams.

Our team consists of passionate students who understand what it's like to prepare for SPM. We've compiled the most relevant and practical notes for:

  • ✍️ Writing (Emails & Essays)
  • 📖 Reading Comprehension & Vocabulary
  • 🗣️ Speaking Test Strategies

All notes are delivered as **images**—perfect for studying on your phone, laptop, or printed handouts. Our goal is to help fellow students pass SPM English with confidence!
